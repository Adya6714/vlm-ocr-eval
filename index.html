Results

What the measurements show

Numbers below follow paper/main.pdf and docs/paper_defensibility_stats.md. Do not treat this page as a second table of record.

The model is not reading

Held-out grapheme CER is 0.985 on text-bearing Hindi and 0.949 on blank (t = 0.257, p = 0.822 after seed clustering). Confidence stays near ceiling on Hindi, blank, Ol Chiki, and Perso-Arabic.

Failure is localised at position 0

Teacher-forced p(ground truth) at the first token is 2.2×10⁻¹¹ (text-bearing) and 6.3×10⁻¹¹ (blank) — 19.8 nats below a text-only n-gram. Median rank of the correct symbol among 367 candidates is 70.5. Self-generated max-softmax is still ≈0.90. Ground truth is never the argmax (0/180).

Mid-sequence the decoder is a 5-gram

Pooled KL(model ‖ 5-gram) at positions 2–9 / 10–19 / 20–39 is 0.32 / 0.27 / 0.28 nats, with argmax agreement 93.5% / 91.2% / 90.7%. At 0, 1, and 40+ both KL and agreement collapse the other way. Three seeds agree. The exclusive “it is that language model” claim is supported.

Noise and scramble do not open a visual gap

Gaussian-noise and patch-scrambled images have whole-sequence mean log p(GT) −1.756 and −1.730, against −1.783 text-bearing and −1.751 blank. All four sit within 0.05 nats. The position-0 collapse repeats on noise (−23.7) and scrambled (−24.1). That is evidence for bypass over a rendering-domain story, not a proof that a visual representation never existed.

AUROC 0.838 is memorised-text only

On synthetic lines sampled from the training manifest, line accuracy is 18.3% and confidence ranks correctness at AUROC 0.838, while mean confidence is 0.994 (ECE ≈ 0.81). Every one of the 300 evaluation instances is in the training manifest. There is no held-out arm to split. On held-out images, accuracy is zero and the signal is useless for review.

2
Method

A blank instrument, then probes

≈19.6M-parameter encoder–decoder, grapheme-cluster vocabulary (|V|≈367), trained from scratch on 2,538 Hindi line crops. No Indic pretraining. Three independent seeds. Scoring applies encoding equivalence before residual error is counted.

Pipeline

GlotOCR pages → Tesseract / Surya / PaddleOCR (encoding-aware scores)
             → renderer (natural / flattened / inverted)
             → train.py → probes → paper figures

The instrument was trained on the full manifest (19 of 60 teacher-forced eval strings appear as training lines). Position 0 still fails at floor; contamination can only have helped there. Mid-sequence likelihood and the synthetic AUROC are not held-out-string results.

What remains

Two open items, not a laundry list

Need new dataAUROC

Held-out synthetic lines

The memorisation split cannot be run on the current Probe 5 set (non-match n=0). Testing whether AUROC tracks correctness vs memorisation needs lines whose text is withheld before training. No new GPU training required for the split itself — new renders, then a new eval.

Need a modelControl

Positive control that actually reads

Surya v0.22 has no exposed softmax. PaddleOCR’s Hindi rec head is CTC, not autoregressive — no teacher-forced p(gᵢ | g₍ᵢ, image). Both candidates failed for architecture, not for lack of trying.

Uniformly averaged token confidence dilutes the one position that carries visual dependence. Weight early tokens.

Why an owned model

A closed API cannot say why confidence stayed high

We trained a ~19.6M reader from scratch (no Indic pretraining) so we can blank the page, zero the encoder, and teacher-force the true first grapheme. That instrument does not read its held-out images. That is what makes the position-0 test interpretable: there is no hidden reading skill, only a language prior that is still ~0.90 confident while p(correct) is ~10⁻¹¹.
